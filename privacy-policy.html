Confident Creator

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Confident Creator (package ID com.confidentcam.confident_cam, formerly developed under the name ConfidentCam, accessible at https://confidentcreator.app/). We are committed to protecting your privacy and explaining clearly what information we collect, how it is processed, and what stays strictly on your device.

This Privacy Policy applies to the Confident Creator Android mobile application and the Confident Creator Web Portal (including the script library, AI script generator, and browser teleprompter).

2. Information We Collect

Account & Authentication Information:

  • Email address, phone number (when phone authentication is used), or Google account sign-in identifier managed through Supabase Authentication
  • Display name and internal account identifier (User ID)

Onboarding & Profile Preferences:

  • First name, age, and city/region location (when provided during onboarding for script personalization)
  • Selected goals, content niche, camera-confidence challenges, experience level, preferred platforms, daily time commitment, timezone, and preferred script language

Scripts, Progress & Activity Data:

  • Custom and AI-generated video scripts (titles, Hook/Body/Close sections, prompt template metadata, and recording status) synced between the mobile app and Web Portal
  • 30-day challenge progress, warmup completion flags, streak counts, daily completion metadata (local video filename reference, duration in seconds, and self-check responses), and optional challenge extensions
  • Reminder preferences (such as reminder enabled status and preferred reminder time)

Web Portal Session & Technical Data:

  • When pairing the mobile app with the Web Portal via QR code, we create a short-lived session record containing a temporary session token, status, expiration timestamp, and browser device metadata (userAgent and platform).
  • Teleprompter visual preferences (font size, scroll speed, colors, and voice mode) are saved locally in your browser's localStorage.
  • When external analytics are enabled by the site configuration, privacy-respecting referral and page-interaction events are recorded while respecting browser Do Not Track (DNT) and Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals.

Local Video Storage Guarantee: Videos you record inside the Confident Creator Android app are stored locally on your device. Confident Creator does not upload your recorded video files to Supabase Storage servers or third-party AI providers. Only non-media completion metadata (such as completion timestamp, duration, and local filename) is synced to track your challenge progress.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Account Access & Cross-Device Sync: To authenticate your account, pair the mobile app with the Web Portal via QR code, and sync your scripts and challenge progress in real time.
  • Personalized Script Generation: To generate speaking practice scripts and creator video scripts tailored to your chosen topic, target audience, tone, length, and preferred language.
  • 30-Day Challenge & Warmup Tracking: To track daily warmups, 30-day challenge milestones, and streaks.
  • Notifications & Support: To deliver user-configured daily practice reminders and respond to support requests.
  • Security & Service Reliability: To maintain service availability, prevent abuse, and verify subscription entitlements when premium features are active.

4. Third-Party Services & Data Processing

We rely on the following third-party infrastructure and service providers:

  • Supabase: Authentication, PostgreSQL database storage, Realtime synchronization, and serverless Edge Functions.
  • OpenAI: Used to generate speaking practice scripts and Creator Studio scripts from the topic, template, tone, length, and personalization inputs you submit. Your recorded videos are never sent to OpenAI.
  • Google Sign-In (OAuth): Used if you choose to authenticate with your Google account in the mobile app.
  • RevenueCat & Google Play Billing (when subscriptions are active): Used to verify purchase receipts and subscription entitlement status. The Confident Creator app is currently free to download on Google Play.
  • GitHub Pages / Fastly CDN: Hosts our static website and Web Portal interface.

5. Web Teleprompter Voice & Microphone Access

In the Web Portal Teleprompter, if you turn on optional Voice Sync mode, the browser requests microphone permission to scroll your script as you speak:

  • Depending on your browser, Voice Sync uses either your browser's Web Speech API (word matching) or local Web Audio API voice-activity detection (VAD).
  • Confident Creator does not record, store, or upload your microphone audio to our servers.

6. Data Storage, Security & Retention

  • Account, profile, progress, and script data are stored in Supabase over HTTPS/TLS connections.
  • QR login sessions automatically expire after 5 minutes if unused or upon logout.
  • We retain your account data for as long as your account remains active.
  • When you request account deletion via the app or support, your authentication account, user profile, progress records, and associated data are scheduled for permanent removal; local videos stored on your phone can also be cleared directly in App Settings or by uninstalling the app.

7. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to request access to your personal data, update inaccurate profile details, export your scripts, or delete your account and associated personal data.

You can delete scripts directly in the Web Portal or mobile app, clear local videos in Settings, use the in-app account deletion option, or email us directly for assistance.

8. Children's Privacy

Confident Creator is not intended for children under 13 years of age (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect product updates or legal requirements. Material updates will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date on this page.
