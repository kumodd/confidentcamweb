Which Teleprompter App Fits Your Stage?

  • Choose Confident Creator if you are new to speaking on camera or want an Android + Desktop Web workflow combining a 30-day confidence challenge, spoken-word AI script generator (including Hinglish and Indian regional languages), lens teleprompter, and local on-device videos at zero cost.
  • Choose BIGVU if you need a full video production suite on iOS/Android with built-in auto-subtitles, transcript trimming, and green-screen/branding overlays.
  • Choose Captions if your top priority is post-recording animated captions, AI eye-contact correction, and generative video editing (especially on iOS).
  • Choose PromptSmart+ if you rely on offline voice-tracking across Windows/macOS Studio or physical glass teleprompter rigs.
  • Choose Teleprompter.com if you want a dedicated multi-platform prompter supporting smartwatch/Bluetooth remotes and up to 4K in-app recording.
